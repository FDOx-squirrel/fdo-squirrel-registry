Cork Ogham Stone CIIC 81 (UCC 4)

3DDataFDO · catalogue record 24287195

3D model of the ogham stone CIIC 81 (CISP GARES/1; University College Cork inv. no. 4), a green sandstone pillar inscribed in Primitive Irish with C[A]SSITT[A]S MAQI MU[CO]I CALLITI. It was unearthed around 1851 in the ringfort at Garranes (An Garrán), Co. Cork (SMR CO084-090003-), and is thought to have come from the souterrain there. Probably acquired for the UCC collection in or around 1907, it is now on permanent display in the Stone Corridor ('Rúin na gCloch / Stories in Stone') at University College Cork, Cork City (SMR CO074-148----).

Found around 1851 in the ringfort at Garranes (SMR CO084-090001-), probably in its souterrain (SMR CO084-090002-); the findspot is recorded as SMR CO084-090003-. Probably acquired for the UCC collection in or around 1907. Now on permanent display in the Stone Corridor of University College Cork, Cork City (SMR CO074-148----, 51.8937, -8.4924), which is the present location, not the findspot. Described in OG(H)AM entry I-COR-030.
