Church Island Ogham Stone CHUIS/1 (Cork Public Museum)

3DDataFDO · catalogue record 24347211

3D model of the ogham stone CHUIS/1 (OG(H)AM I-KER-136; CISP CHUIS/1), a cross slab from Church Island (Oileán an Teampaill), Co. Kerry, now in the Cork Public Museum (inventory number 1777.1023, G1094). The slab measures 1.49 x 0.36 x 0.10 m and is fractured at its base. Its ogham inscription, read along the arris as BECCDINN MACI RIVVOESS, is in Primitive Irish, and a compass-drawn, incised ringed cross of Maltese type was added later; the excavator dates this design to 650-750 AD. The stone was found in a secondary position in the south-western quadrant of the island. The model was made with KIRI Engine from 77 photographs taken with a smartphone.

Found at Church Island (Oileán an Teampaill), Co. Kerry (SMR KE079-032004-), in a secondary position in the south-western quadrant of the island; now displayed in the Cork Public Museum, Cork (51.896085, -8.494222), inventory number 1777.1023 (G1094). Findspot and present location are different places; the spatial point of this FDO is the findspot. Described in OG(H)AM entry I-KER-136; CISP CHUIS/1; no CIIC number is listed.
