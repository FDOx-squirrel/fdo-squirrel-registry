Gowran: Ogham Stone

3DDataFDO · catalogue record 24285299

3D model of the ogham stone from Gowran (Gabhrán), Co. Kilkenny, Ireland (CIIC 32; CISP GOWRN/1; SMR KK020-060007-), a grit pillar stone inscribed in Primitive Irish. It was found in the foundations of the chancel of the medieval parish church of St Mary's, Gowran, when the chancel was demolished in 1827, and is now displayed on site in the rebuilt chancel. The model was made with KIRI Engine from 111 photographs; the uploader notes that the scan is imperfect because of poor lighting on site.

Found in the foundations of the chancel of St Mary's, Gowran (SMR KK020-060006-), demolished in 1827; the inscription was first noticed in 1849. Now on site in the rebuilt chancel. Described in OG(H)AM entry I-KIK-007.
