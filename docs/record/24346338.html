Govan 2 Hogback (Govan Stones)

3DDataFDO · catalogue record 24346338

3D model of Govan 2 (Stirling Maxwell and ECMS numbering; Lang 1), one of the five hogback grave monuments of Govan Old Parish Church in Glasgow. At about two metres in length it is the smallest of the five and the best preserved, and it is dated to the early 10th century. Above two rows of tegulation (roof-shingle carving) the 3D analysis shows what appears to be a row of step pattern along the worn ridge. The model was recorded by structure-from-motion photogrammetry as part of Megan N. Kasten's PhD research on the Govan Stones (University of Glasgow, 2019) and was published on Sketchfab by the Govan Stones account under CC BY 4.0.

Early medieval hogback grave monument from Govan Old (Govan Old Parish Church, Govan Road, Glasgow), where the five Govan hogbacks were found; now displayed in the Govan Stones museum in the church. Numbered Govan 2 by Stirling Maxwell and in the ECMS, Lang 1 in Lang's corpus. Smallest of the five hogbacks; Lang and Bailey suggest influences from Cumbria or further afield in its form and in the decoration along its lower edge.
