Lego Pyramid (Reconstruction claude.ai)

3DDataFDO · catalogue record 24305344

Idealised reconstruction of a white stepped LEGO pyramid (nine levels) with a five-brick column and a minifigure-style warrior figure on top (spiked helmet, mail shirt, tunic, cloak), a hobby build by Florian Thiery, rebuilt procedurally from clean LEGO bricks after a KIRI Engine scan. Only dimensions and structure were read from the scan; no scan mesh or texture was reused. AI disclosure: the reconstruction was generated with Claude (Anthropic, claude-sonnet-5-5) on behalf of Florian Thiery. It is an interpretation model, not a measurement.

Self-built (own design, no commercial set) by Florian Thiery. Shown at the Apothekertrakt of Schönbrunn Palace during CHNT31, 2026-11-12 (https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/6892072). The figure on top is read as a medieval warrior; that and any further identification are open hypotheses, not confirmed.
